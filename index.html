Transcription that stays on your machine.

Vox Scripta turns recordings into transcripts without sending them anywhere. Recognition, tidying and polishing all run on your own device.

Point it at an interview, a meeting, a lecture or a voice memo — any length — and it writes out what was said. It works on a plane, in a basement, on a machine that has never been online.

  • Nothing is uploaded. There is no server to send audio to. The Android build does not even ask for internet permission, which you can check on the listing yourself.
  • No account is required. Nothing to sign up for before you can use it.
  • No telemetry. No usage data, no crash pings, no analytics.
  • Your recording is never altered. The original file is read and never written to, whatever else the app does.

Two readings of every recording

Verbatim

Exactly what was said, hesitations and repetitions included. A record, not a rewrite.

Polished

The same words made readable, sentence by sentence. Every rewrite is checked against the original and discarded if it changes the meaning — and you can see which ones were refused and why.

Export either one as plain text, Markdown, Word, or as subtitles in SRT or VTT. Reading and exporting what you have already transcribed is never locked, including after a subscription ends.

In English and Spanish

The interface and the app's own messages are available in both. Recordings can be transcribed in either language, and the app can work out which one it is hearing — and tell you when the audio does not sound like the language it was asked for.
