Vox Scripta

Transcription that happens on your own machine.

Vox Scripta turns recordings into transcripts without sending them anywhere. Recognition, cleanup and polishing all run on your device. There is no server, no account required, no telemetry, and no upload — your audio never leaves the machine you play it on.
