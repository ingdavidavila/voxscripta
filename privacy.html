Privacy Policy

Vox Scripta, published by Working Robots.

Last updated 4 November 2026.

The short version

Vox Scripta transcribes audio on your own device. Your recordings and your transcripts are never uploaded, never sent to a server, and never seen by us. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

We do not collect your personal information. The rest of this policy explains that in the detail the law expects, and is specific about the few places the app touches a network at all.

What we collect

Nothing.

That is not a simplification. Vox Scripta has no server. There is no account to create, no profile, no identifier assigned to you, and nowhere for your data to go even if we wanted it.

What stays on your device

Your audioRead, never modified, never uploaded. The app never writes to the file you give it.
Your transcriptsSaved in a project folder on your own disk, under your control.
TranscriptionPerformed entirely on your device.
Silence removal and cleanupPerformed entirely on your device, by fixed signal processing.
PolishingPerformed entirely on your device, by a language model that ships with the app.
Your settingsStored on your device.

No part of this requires an internet connection, and none of it sends anything anywhere.

When the app does use the network

Two cases, and only two.

Purchases. If you subscribe, the purchase is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. Your payment details go to Apple or Google and never reach this app or any system of ours. We receive no card number, no billing address and no name. What the app learns is whether a subscription is active. Apple and Google each publish their own privacy policies covering that transaction.

Nothing else. Some editions of Vox Scripta are built with no network capability at all, enforced by the operating system rather than by our promise.

Accounts

Vox Scripta works fully without an account, and the app never requires one to transcribe.

If a future version offers an optional account, it will store your email address and nothing else, solely so you can sign in. It will not be sold, shared, or used for advertising, and you will be able to delete the account and everything attached to it from inside the app. This policy will be updated before any such feature ships.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal information. We have none to sell.
  • We do not share your information with advertisers, data brokers or anyone else.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not use your recordings or transcripts to train any model.
  • We do not require you to identify yourself to use the app.

Children

Vox Scripta is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. Since the app collects nothing from any user, no information about a child can be collected through it.

Your rights

Residents of California, and of other states with similar laws, have rights to know what personal information is collected about them, to have it deleted, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights.

Because Vox Scripta collects no personal information, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete. Everything the app produces is already in your possession, on your own device, and you can delete it the way you delete any other file. If you believe we hold information about you, write to us and we will respond.

Security

Your recordings and transcripts are protected by the security of your own device. We recommend using your device's disk encryption and screen lock, as those are what actually guard this data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. If a change ever means the app begins collecting something, that change will be described plainly and in advance, not buried.
